# CUSHION Phase 0 — Drift Log

Record every incorrect assumption, documentation mismatch, unexpected SDK/API behavior, workaround, or product change required by technical reality.

Phase 0.5C corrections and discoveries:

- Phase 0.5H settlement tracking: market `0x...9a4f` remained Trading, unfinalized, unresolved, and non-voided with `24731` seconds to expiry. Wallet NO ownership remained `1000` raw and nothing was claimable. The on-chain helper's `winningOutcome=0` is an empty-vector default while unresolved, not evidence of a YES winner; gate winner display on resolved/voided state.

- Phase 0.5G execution proof: exact approved transaction `0xe6641626c5183747e1fe03ae72cca4925f5ca72e6fbdea141822cc8c8d157412` fully filled `1000` raw BUY_NO at effective NO `0.802000`, spent `802` raw tUSDC, and increased the wallet's NO balance by `1000`. DreamDEX `BinaryOrderPlaced`, `OrderFilled`, and `OrderPlaced` logs verified the native path. Finalization/redemption remain pending.
- Finite allowance accounting differs from net execution cost: the pool consumed the `900`-raw maximum escrow allowance, then returned `98` raw collateral price improvement. Allowance fell from `722000` to `721100` even though net tUSDC spend was `802`. Preflight future orders against gross maximum escrow, not expected/net fill cost.

- Phase 0.5F bounded execution: a fresh exact simulation passed at maximum NO `0.752000`, but the authorized pre-broadcast refresh returned `0.780000`. The safety guard stopped before signing (`BROADCAST_OCCURRED=NO`). A fixed ceiling bounds cost but does not eliminate non-execution when price movement exceeds the approved buffer.

- Phase 0.5D broadcast guard: the approved BTC 24h BUY_NO IOC at NO `0.783000` was no longer executable when the final book refresh returned `0.793000`. The utility stopped before signing (`BROADCAST_OCCURRED=NO`). A replacement requires wire price `207000`, cost `793` raw tUSDC for quantity `1000`, a fresh expiry/calldata, resimulation, and new approval.

- The Phase 0.5B ETH 15m result was selection drift, not proof that approved BTC 24h `0x...9a4f` rolled away. BTC 24h remained Trading on pool `0xC3F0...5cc8`, nonce 130, with allowance 722000. Persist and revalidate the bound market ID; never select the first rediscovered row.
- Direct buys approve the selected BinaryPool. Fifteen pool addresses were observed across 200 finalized rows and were recycled across assets, windows, and nonces. This is not a guaranteed protocol maximum.
- Direct EIP-2612 and direct-order Permit2 are not supported. Permit2 is complete-set-mint-only. `placeBinaryOrderFor` delegation and atomic approve/order remain `UNVERIFIED`; ERC-6909 operator grants apply to sells.
- Pool-address allowance can survive generations only while value remains. Finite approvals are consumed by collateral transfer; the installed SDK instead defaults to unlimited approval. Re-read allowance and disclose the security/convenience tradeoff.
